# From Cables to Cloud

**A new podcast chat about the fast-changing world of network and infrastructure engineering**

---

The latest episode of the IT Infrastructure Podcast is live. This time I sat down with **Sander Jansen**, ICT Consultant, to talk about what modern IT infrastructure really looks like in day-to-day work. No buzzwords, no polished sales pitches — just honest, practical insights from the field.

## What will you hear in this episode?

We take you through six segments that paint the full picture: from Sander's personal career path to the major strategic questions many organizations face today.

**Legacy vs. modernization** - How do you keep moving forward while older systems still need to stay online? Sander shares a down-to-earth perspective on the pressure every infrastructure team knows.

**Hybrid cloud in practice** - What brochures promise versus what you actually encounter. We dive into visibility challenges and troubleshooting across on-prem and cloud environments.

**AI/ML and network design** - How are AI workloads changing the way you design infrastructure and plan capacity? And where does Azure fit into the bigger picture?

**War stories** - A project that went off the rails, what exactly went wrong, and what Sander learned from it. Plus: how do you make the case for infrastructure investments to non-technical management?

**The future of the profession** - Is the network engineer role disappearing, or becoming more important than ever? And what advice would Sander give to someone just starting out in infrastructure?
