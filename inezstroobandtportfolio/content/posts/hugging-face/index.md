On October 28, 2025, I attended a Tech&Meet evening at Howest, organized by **Cyber3Lab**. It was a two-hour session entirely dedicated to **open-source AI** and the **Hugging Face ecosystem**. This fits into the series of Tech&Meet evenings that Cyber3Lab regularly organizes for students and professionals.

The speaker was **Niels Rogge**, and his profile was genuinely impressive. He works as a machine learning engineer at **ML6**, a Belgian AI consultancy that implements generative AI solutions for large companies in the Benelux, Germany, and beyond. In addition, he works part-time at **Hugging Face**, where he is part of the open-source team. He has a special interest in **NLP** and contributes to open-source libraries such as the Hugging Face Transformers library, mainly working with deep learning models, PyTorch, and Google Colab. On X (Twitter), he now has more than 20,000 followers, which shows that he is a well-known voice in the international ML community. In short: someone with a lot of hands-on experience and a clear connection to the tools he spoke about.

The event was clearly technical in nature, but remained accessible to a broad audience: from beginners curious about AI to developers already working with machine learning. The focus on Hugging Face as a platform was a smart choice, because it is one of the most approachable entry points into open-source AI, with ready-to-use models, datasets, and tools.

What I learned included the difference between open-source and closed-source AI, and how organizations can use both. That nuance is often missing in education: we hear a lot about large commercial models like GPT, but the open-source alternatives and their practical applications get less attention. This event filled that gap in a meaningful way.

Would I attend this event again? Definitely. The combination of a strong speaker, relevant content, and the chance to network afterward makes it well worth it. I would also recommend it to fellow students, even to those without a technical background in AI: that broad accessibility is exactly what makes evenings like this valuable.
