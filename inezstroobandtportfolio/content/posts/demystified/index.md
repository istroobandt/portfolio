On November 18, 2025, I attended the **.NET 10 Demystified** session, hosted on the Howest campus in Bruges. It was a one-off two-hour evening session for developers and students who wanted to understand what the newest version of the .NET platform actually changes and what that means in practice.

The speaker was **Kevin DeRudder**, a familiar name in the Belgian .NET community. He is a full-stack web developer with a focus on **ASP.NET** and works as a trainer and coach. He is also one of the organizers behind **Techorama**, one of the largest developer conferences in Belgium and the Netherlands. He also speaks regularly at international conferences such as **NDC**. It quickly becomes clear that he not only has a lot of technical knowledge, but also knows how to explain it clearly.

The session focused on the most important new features in **.NET 10**: improvements in C#, updates to the runtime and libraries, tooling improvements, and better support for cloud-native applications. Kevin clearly wanted to separate the hype from the essentials, and he succeeded. The session was technical, but still easy to follow, even if you do not work with .NET every day.

What stayed with me most was the emphasis on practical applicability. Kevin illustrated each point with examples that fit realistic projects, such as enterprise applications, microservices, and AI integrations in .NET. That makes a real difference compared with a dry list of changelogs. You leave with insights you can use the next day.

The format of a short, focused evening session works well. Two hours is enough to explore a topic in depth without getting overwhelmed, and the location on the home campus lowers the barrier to attend. I would recommend this session to anyone who already works with .NET or plans to do so soon. I would definitely attend a future edition again myself.
