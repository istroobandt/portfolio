## Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science student at Howest who genuinely enjoys building things with code. I care about understanding *why* things work the way they do, not just making them work. For me, good software isn't just about clever code—it's about solving real problems for real people.

## What Gets Me Excited

I'm curious about where development is heading. I want to understand how cross-platform development actually works in practice, and what it really means when people say "write once, run everywhere." I'm genuinely interested in AI—not because it's trendy, but because I want to understand what it means for how we work and what responsibilities come with it. Full-stack development fascinates me because it forces you to see the bigger picture. And cloud-native stuff? That's about building things that actually work at scale.

## How I Learn Best

Classroom learning is fine, but I learn fastest by doing. That's why I jump into hackathons like **Hack The Future 2025** and the **Vibe Coding Hackathon**—building something real under pressure teaches you things no lecture can. I also regularly attend Cyber3Lab's **Tech&Meet evenings** because, honestly, hearing from people actually working in the field beats any textbook. Those conversations, the "oh, that's how it works in practice" moments, stick with me.

## What Actually Matters

I want to be the kind of developer who asks good questions—not to be difficult, but because I genuinely want to understand if we're solving the right problem. Code quality and security matter to me because I know that cutting corners creates real headaches down the line. And working with good people who communicate clearly? That's worth more than being the smartest person in the room.
